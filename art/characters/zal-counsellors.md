## Prompt line

Zal's learned counsellors are three small adult manuscript men: an elderly white-bearded man in an ivory robe and plain lapis turban, a middle-aged short-black-bearded man in a muted grey robe and ivory turban, and a younger clean-shaven man in a dark lapis robe and ivory cap.
