# Zal's letter courier · پیک نامه‌ی زال

The anonymous adult male rider who carries Zal's letter to Sam and brings
the answer back in 08.13. Distinct from the woman messenger of 08.14.

## Prompt line

Zal's anonymous adult male letter courier at small Persian manuscript scale, with a narrow olive face, dark almond eyes, dark hair mostly covered by a plain ivory headcloth and a short dark beard. A lapis-blue riding coat with muted ochre floral trim over an ivory tunic and trousers, a plain dark belt and soft dark boots. No crown, no armour, no halo and no weapon raised. He carries a completely blank folded ivory letter or a plain dark closed pouch. His main horse is dark brown and his spare horse is dun; both have modest unlettered tack and stay inside the central safe area when shown.
