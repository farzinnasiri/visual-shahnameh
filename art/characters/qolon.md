# Qolon · قلون

Turanian captain sent by Afrasiyab to block Rostam and Kay Qobad near Alborz.

## Prompt line

Qolon, Afrasiyab's Turanian captain, a lean adult at small Persian manuscript scale. A long angular face, dark almond eyes, a thin black moustache and a short pointed beard. Dark malachite lamellar armour over an ivory tunic, a dull indigo short cloak, and a plain iron cap without plume. He carries one long spear with a plain steel point and rides a narrow dark-brown horse. No crown, farr, horns or giant stature. He faces the viewer's left when opposing Rostam.
