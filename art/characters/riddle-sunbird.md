# The sun bird · مرغ نمادین خورشید

A small symbolic bird between the two cypresses in 08.24, distinct from
the Simurgh. No celestial person or religious figure is depicted.

## Prompt line

The riddle's sun bird is one tiny ordinary manuscript bird with dull shell-gold feathers, a short lapis-blue tail, a small ivory beak and one dark eye. Two natural wings and two feet, no human face, crown, halo or flaming body. It moves between the two slender cypress trees as an unlettered seasonal symbol. Its gold is always dimmer than the central landscape glint.

## Notes

- 2026-11-06: This is not the Simurgh and has no huge crest or long
  vermilion-tipped phoenix tail.
