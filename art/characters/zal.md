# Zal · زال

Son of Sam, raised by the Simurgh. First appears in 08.02 as an infant, grows
in 08.03, and returns to Sam in 08.04. His white hair is human, never a sign
of a div. Do not confuse him with his father Sam or his son Rostam.

## Prompt line

Paste this unchanged into every prompt where this figure appears; state the
age of the scene separately.

Zal, son of Sam, a human of unusually fair skin, dark almond eyes with white eyelashes, vermilion lips and a straight nose. His hair is pure ivory-white from birth, never blond, silver-grey or dark. At infant age he is a small unarmed baby with a soft tuft of white hair; at youth age he is tall and broad-shouldered with a narrow waist, no beard and long white hair falling below his arms. In the nest his clothing is only a simple ivory wrap; after Sam clothes him he wears an ivory tunic beneath a turquoise champion's coat with a narrow gold belt. No halo, wings, claws or animal features. A crown or weapon appears only when the source scene grants it. All figures remain small and flat at Persian manuscript scale.
