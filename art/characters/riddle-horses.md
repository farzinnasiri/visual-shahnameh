# The two riddle horses · دو اسب راز شب و روز

Symbolic horses in the sages' riddle and Zal's answer, not real mounts or
named warhorses. Their contrast represents the night and day.

## Prompt line

The riddle horses are two tiny ordinary manuscript horses, one ivory-white with a small muted lapis-blue saddlecloth and one lamp-black with a small muted wine-red saddlecloth. Plain dull-gold bridles, no riders, armour, wings, horns or halos. Their flowing tails and curved legs suggest an endless circular chase without touching or fighting. Both remain compact in the central landscape, their cloth and tack much dimmer than the central light.
