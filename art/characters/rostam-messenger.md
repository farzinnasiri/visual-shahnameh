# Messenger of Rostam's birth

Anonymous courier carrying the silk effigy and reply in 09.29. A recurring visual role, without asserting identity with an earlier unnamed messenger.

## Prompt line

The anonymous messenger of Rostam's birth is a small adult human man with an olive oval face, dark almond eyes, a short neat black beard and black hair covered by a plain ivory headcloth. He wears a muted plum travel coat over an ivory tunic, a narrow ochre sash and dark trousers. No crown, armour, jewellery, halo or drawn weapon. His gift tray or sealed blank letter remains small and dim beside the central lamp.
