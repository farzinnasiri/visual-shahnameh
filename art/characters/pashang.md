# Pashang · پشنگ

Father of Afrasiyab and the murdered Aghrirath. He refuses to meet Afrasiyab, then urges an attack on Iran's vacant throne by message.

## Prompt line

Pashang, elder ruler of Turan and father of Afrasiyab, a small Persian manuscript figure. A narrow lined face, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a full white beard divided into two tapered points. A muted malachite robe over ivory, a plain dark-gold belt, and a low rounded indigo cap with no jewel. No weapon, farr or tall crown. He faces the viewer's left. In scenes of refusal he turns his body away from Afrasiyab without changing the left-facing profile.
