# Rudabeh's five attendants · پنج پرستندهٔ رودابه

The five adult women who keep Rudabeh's secret, gather flowers by the river,
carry messages and prepare the meeting. They are people, not magical beings;
their talk of flying and enchantment in 08.08 is a pledge, not literal magic.

## Prompt line

Rudabeh's five adult women attendants, all small Persian manuscript figures with dark almond eyes and long black hair gathered into modest braids. They wear plain long-sleeved ivory dresses beneath five distinct muted outer robes: saffron, dusty rose, violet, turquoise and lapis, each with a narrow gold belt and a small ivory headcloth. No crowns, armour, weapons, wings or halos. Their faces are individually varied but understated; the saffron-robed woman is the usual spokeswoman, with one open palm. They remain a compact group, no portrait-scale faces, and their jewellery is duller than the central lamp.
