# Birth healer

Anonymous skilled healer assisting at Rostam's birth, first painted in 08.29. A continuity choice for the recurring role; the source does not name him.

## Prompt line

The anonymous birth healer is a small mature human man with an olive narrow face, short dark-grey beard and dark eyes, an ivory wrapped headcloth, a plain muted ochre long robe over an ivory tunic and a dark narrow belt. His hands are calm and clean. No crown, armour, weapon, halo or modern medical equipment. He remains a tiny background figure beside the household, never a portrait.
