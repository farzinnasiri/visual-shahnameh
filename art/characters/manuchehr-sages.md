# Manuchehr's questioning sages · موبدان پرسشگر منوچهر

The three anonymous wise men who test Zal through riddles in 09.23. They
are human court scholars, distinct from Zal's own camp counsellors.

## Prompt line

Manuchehr's three questioning sages are tiny elderly manuscript men with thoughtful dark almond eyes, long white beards and plain ivory turbans. Their muted long robes are respectively lapis-blue over ivory, dark wine over ivory, and malachite-green over ivory, each with a narrow plain cloth belt. No crown, halo, weapon, sacred insignia or readable scroll. The lapis-robed elder often opens one palm to ask a question; their gestures stay restrained. They remain a compact small court group, never portrait-scale faces.
