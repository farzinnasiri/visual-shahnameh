# Sindokht · سیندخت

Queen of Kabul, wife of Mehrab and mother of Rudabeh. Her intelligence and
restraint turn the household crisis into diplomacy. First painted in 09.07.

## Prompt line

Sindokht, a mature adult queen at small Persian manuscript scale, with an olive oval face, thoughtful dark almond eyes, fine dark arched eyebrows, a straight nose and a small closed mouth. Her black hair is gathered beneath an ivory headcloth edged with a narrow malachite band. A deep turquoise long-sleeved robe over an ivory dress, a dark lapis shawl around her shoulders, a narrow plain gold belt and small pearl earrings. No tall crown, no armour, no weapon and no halo. Her gestures are composed and deliberate, with one open palm when counselling or negotiating. She is distinct from young Rudabeh's two long black braids and malachite robe. All jewellery is muted, dimmer than the painting's central light.
