# Rakhsh's mother · مادر رخش

The fiercely protective mare in 13.04.

## Prompt line

Rakhsh's mother, a swift mare at small flat Persian manuscript scale: pale ivory-white coat, a broad lion-like chest, short strong legs, black eyes, two sharply pointed ears, and a flowing cream mane. She is plainly larger and paler than her saffron-gold red-roan foal Rakhsh. No saddle, reins, brand, wings or horn. Her anger is shown by her lowered head and moving hooves, never by a monstrous face.
