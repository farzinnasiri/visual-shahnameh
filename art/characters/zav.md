# Zav · زو

Son of Tahmasp, king of chapter 10. His name appears in the chapter title as زوطهماسپ and in the section title as زوتهماسپ. Not Garshasp or Sam.

## Prompt line

Zav, son of Tahmasp, an eighty-year-old Iranian king at miniature scale. A narrow lined face, dark almond eyes, a straight long nose, a short forked ivory-white beard and white hair to the jaw. A low plain gold diadem with one small turquoise oval, never a tall crown. A malachite-green robe over an ivory tunic, a muted saffron mantle, a plain gold belt. Slender and slightly stooped but alert. No weapon in his hands. A faint pale-gold farr stays dimmer than the central lamp. He always faces the viewer's left.
