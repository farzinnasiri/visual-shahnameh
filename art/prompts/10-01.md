Ten couplets in chapter 09. Four paintings: the empty threshold, Nowzar’s accession, his unjust turn, and revolt with the summons to Sam. ## Phone composition

Upstream issue #17 found that a 390×844 phone initially exposes only about 27.5% of a landscape painting’s width, with less visible during push-in. Place faces, hands and plot objects in a compact central group above the verse panel. The centre must contain a real painted light for the portal. Inspect first and last couplet frames in the real reader.

## Character continuity

Nowzar, a young adult royal envoy at small manuscript scale, with an oval face, dark almond eyes, a long straight nose, a small closed mouth and a short neat dark beard. Dark hair to his jaw beneath a plain rounded silver helmet without a plume or crest. An indigo short riding coat over an ivory tunic, a narrow turquoise sash and a plain gold belt. A straight sword remains sheathed. No crown, no royal diadem, no ox-head mace and no farr. He rides a small chestnut horse with an indigo saddlecloth and plain dark tack; horse and rider remain fully inside the central safe area.

That established line describes the earlier envoy. In 10.01, his inherited narrow turquoise diadem replaces the helmet. His face, beard, indigo coat and turquoise sash stay the same.

Persian miniature painting, Timurid–Safavid manuscript style (Herat/Tabriz school, 15th–16th century), opaque mineral pigments and shell gold on burnished paper, fine brushwork, flat stacked perspective, no Western shading. Night palette: deep lapis-lazuli and ultramarine, glowing gold leaf, small vermilion accents, malachite and turquoise, ivory. Visible paper grain and slight gold craquelure. SUBJECT: An empty ivory throne and narrow turquoise royal diadem on a cushion remember Manuchehr after his death. A small suspended oil lamp glows over the throne in a lapis-night garden with gold chi clouds, cypresses and spongy rocks. No people, corpse or tomb. COMPOSITION: The white-gold hanging lamp flame is the brightest element at the image centre. The throne and diadem are small below it, still near the central axis. Outer garden is decorative. Full-bleed landscape 3:2. Strictly no text, letters, calligraphy, numerals, border, frame, margin or signature. No modern objects, no divine figure, no photorealism, no 3D.

Review: The first two candidates put the lamp too low. The third put it at the centre; accepted. Original 4:3 JPEG was centre-cropped and resized to 1536×1024 PNG before AVIF conversion.
