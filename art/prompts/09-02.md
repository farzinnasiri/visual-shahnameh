# 09.02 · نامه نوشتن نوذر به نزدیک سام — illustration prompts

Fifty couplets, eleven paintings. The letter moves from Nowzar’s prayer and appeal to Sam’s muster, the nobles’ proposed usurpation, Sam’s refusal and his restoration of the king. Sam’s loyalty is the moral centre.

## Composition

The current portrait reader gives the entire painting a separate measured area above the verse card. Keep faces and plot objects in the central 45% width; put the brightest portal light at image centre. Review 320×568, 390×844 and desktop frames before expanding the chapter batch.
