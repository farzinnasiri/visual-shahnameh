# 08.04 · آگاه شدن سام نریمان، از زال

## Arc

News and two dreams awaken Sam’s conscience. His journey reaches an impossible nest; the Simurgh persuades Zal to leave, gives a feather, and returns him to his father. The homecoming earns its light through remorse.

All scenes belong to chapter `08 · منوچهر`. Source text: `text/chapters`.
Each central light leads into the next painting; no frame, text or religious figure.
