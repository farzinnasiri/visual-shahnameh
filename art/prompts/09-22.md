# 09.22 پرسیدن منوچهر، اختر زال را از اخترماران

## Arc

Paintings follow the source couplets in order. A quiet threshold opens onto successive central lights; reported memories and prophecies remain distinct from the present audience.

## 0 · 00-akhtar.png

Couplets: Threshold (no couplets)

Shows: چراغ و ابزار ساده‌ی اخترشماری در بارگاه

Persian miniature painting, Timurid–Safavid manuscript style (Herat/Tabriz school, 15th–16th century), opaque mineral pigments and shell gold on burnished paper, fine brushwork, flat stacked perspective, no Western shading. Night palette: deep lapis-lazuli and ultramarine, glowing gold leaf, small vermilion accents, malachite and turquoise, ivory. Visible paper grain and slight gold craquelure.
SUBJECT: Empty dark observatory-like palace recess, a plain geometric astrolabe and rolled parchment beside a central lamp, lapis sky with tiny dull gold rosettes. No modern astronomy and no letters.

COMPOSITION: A single tiny white-gold lamp or glint, the brightest element, sits at the exact image centre (768,512). Put it on a raised shelf or rock at that height, never at the bottom. All important figures and narrative objects remain inside central 45% width, x=423..1113. People are small outlined manuscript figures, no more than a quarter of image height; pull far back. All other gold and moons are dull ochre, darker than the central flame. Dim cypress and plane trees, spongy coloured rocks, chi cloud bands and eslimi foliage extend off every edge. Full-bleed landscape3:2, exactly1536x1024. Strictly no text, letters, calligraphy, numerals, inscriptions, border, frame, margin, cartouche or signature. Pages show only plain geometric decoration. No depiction of God, the Prophet or the Imams; sky contains no divine face, hand, eye, body or writing. Not a3D render, not photographic, not fantasy concept art.

Review: Accepted visual review: empty astrologers’ threshold with plain geometric instrument and rolled charts, no glyphs or numerals, central lamp and lapis night.
